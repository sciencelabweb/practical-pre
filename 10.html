Micrometer Screw Gauge

Theory & Principle

Measurement of small linear dimensions using the pitch ($x$) and circular scale divisions ($n$).

Least Count (LC) = Pitch (x) / Circular Divisions (n) = 0.5 mm / 50 = 0.01 mm
